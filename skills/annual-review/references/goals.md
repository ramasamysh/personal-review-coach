# Choose goals for the planning year

1. Load the user's approved reflections, wishes and relevant evidence ledger entries, only inside the chosen workspace. Distinguish evidence-backed observations from preferences and hypotheses.
2. Ask which directions matter most; encourage 3–5 active priorities, but the user decides. Record candidates separately from selected goals.
3. Include `Stop / do less`, `Defer / someday`, and constraints of time, energy and resources. Invite the user to name trade-offs rather than assuming them.
4. Never represent a promotion, other person's behavior or external outcome as under the user's complete control.
5. For **selected user-approved goals only**, allocate unique `G-<PLANNING_YEAR>-NNN` IDs. Add approved current snapshots in `goals.md` and append `created` events in `goal-history.md` according to [goal-history](goal-history.md). If already present, reuse IDs and avoid duplicate creation events.
6. Ask for approval of any quantitative target, baseline or process cadence. Unknowns remain `TBD`, never invented.
7. Before saving, reread both affected files and verify consistency. Do not overwrite candidate drafts or manual edits.
