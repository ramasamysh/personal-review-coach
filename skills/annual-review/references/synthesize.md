# Synthesize — grounded annual review

Read the approved `reflection.md`, `wishes.md`, `goals.md`, `evidence.md`, `goal-history.md`, `index.md` and **only** relevant approved comparisons if present. Prepare a concise synthesis including:

- Accomplishments and difficulties, each marked as `self-reported` or `documented` when applicable, citing local file+heading and `EV-*` ID if available.
- Themes supported by two or more passages, without diagnosing personality or inventing causal stories.
- Tensions and unknowns explicitly framed as hypotheses.
- Selected goals and goal IDs, including stop/defer decisions and the next small actions.
- Evidence gaps and potential next-quarter check-in questions.

Draft `synthesis.md` only after user consents; if it exists, present proposed modifications and preserve prior approved text. Do not mark the annual review complete based solely on synthesis file existence.
