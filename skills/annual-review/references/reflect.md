# Reflect — events, interpretations and evidence

Work one user-selected category at a time, with 1–2 questions per turn. Possible prompts:

- What 2–3 concrete moments mattered? Which are observations and which are interpretations?
- What went well, and how do you know? You can rely on personal recollection; documents are optional.
- What was difficult? What was controllable or outside your control?
- What energized or depleted you? What did you learn?
- Were there prior process commitments? Compare the stated plan with what the user remembers doing, without inventing success percentages.

Write only user-approved content to `reflection.md` under the relevant category. Organize `Observed events`, `User interpretation`, `Lessons`, `Open questions`. For claims suitable for tracking, **offer**, rather than require, an `EV-*` ledger record using [evidence-ledger](evidence-ledger.md). Never treat a source-less self-report as independently verified. If a user corrects a claim, record the correction with their permission and keep linked evidence consistent.
